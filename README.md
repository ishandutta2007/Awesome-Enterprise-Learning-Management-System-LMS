# Awesome-Enterprise-Learning-Management-System-LMS

## Top Enterprise Learning Management System (LMS) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Corporate Training, Academic Learning & Open-Source Education Platforms*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Enterprise Learning Management Systems (LMS)**. These tools manage, deliver, and track online training, courses, assessments, compliance, and learner progress across organizations.



**Examples** include Adobe Learning Manager, Docebo, Cornerstone OnDemand, Absorb LMS, SAP Litmos, TalentLMS, LearnUpon, 360Learning, Blackboard Learn, and Canvas LMS (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom integrations, and open education infrastructure — ideal for universities, enterprises, training providers, and developers building transparent, sovereign learning platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Adobe Learning Manager](https://learningmanager.adobe.com/)**  

  Enterprise LMS from Adobe with AI-powered recommendations, gamification, and integrations with Adobe ecosystem.



- **[Docebo](https://www.docebo.com/)**  

  AI-powered learning platform for employee, customer, and partner training with extended enterprise portals.



- **[Cornerstone OnDemand](https://www.cornerstoneondemand.com/)**  

  Comprehensive talent and learning suite with skills tracking, compliance, and performance management.



- **[Absorb LMS](https://www.absorblms.com/)**  

  Intuitive LMS with strong reporting, e-commerce, and content integrations for corporate training.



- **[SAP Litmos](https://www.litmos.com/)**  

  Rapid-deployment LMS with built-in content library, compliance courses, and mobile learning.



- **[TalentLMS](https://www.talentlms.com/)**  

  Cloud LMS known for ease of use, unlimited courses on most plans, and quick setup for small to mid-sized teams.



- **[LearnUpon](https://www.learnupon.com/)**  

  LMS focused on customer, partner, and employee training with multi-portal support and strong automation.



- **[360Learning](https://360learning.com/)**  

  Collaborative learning platform emphasizing user-generated content and AI-powered skills development.



- **[Blackboard Learn](https://www.blackboard.com/)**  

  Established academic LMS widely used in higher education with assessment, collaboration, and analytics tools.



- **[Canvas LMS](https://www.instructure.com/canvas)**  

  Modern academic LMS with an open-source core, popular in higher education for its clean interface and workflow .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Moodle](https://github.com/moodle/moodle)**  

  The world's most widely deployed open-source LMS, with 2,000+ plugins, SCORM/LTI compliance, and use in 240+ countries .



- **[Open edX](https://github.com/openedx/edx-platform)**  

  MOOC-scale platform powering edX.org, built by MIT and Harvard; excels at video-based courses and auto-graded coding exercises .



- **[Canvas LMS (Open Source Core)](https://github.com/instructure/canvas-lms)**  

  The open-source core of Canvas, offering a modern interface and robust academic features for institutions willing to self-host .



- **[Chamilo](https://github.com/chamilo/chamilo-lms)**  

  Lightweight LMS focused on ease of use, with built-in learning paths, video conferencing integration, and modest resource requirements .



- **[ILIAS](https://github.com/ILIAS-eLearning/ILIAS)**  

  Feature-rich German-engineered LMS with strong assessment, competency tracking, and compliance training capabilities .



- **[Sakai](https://github.com/sakaiproject/sakai)**  

  Community-developed learning and collaboration suite widely used in higher education and research institutions .



- **[Totara](https://github.com/totara/totara)**  

  Enterprise-focused fork of Moodle with certifications, compliance reporting, organizational hierarchies, and multitenancy .



- **[Richie](https://github.com/openfun/richie)**  

  Open-source CMS for building education portals that aggregate courses from multiple LMS instances, by France Université Numérique .



- **[Pupilfirst](https://github.com/pupilfirst/pupilfirst)**  

  LMS for running asynchronous online schools with a focus on tasks, feedback, and community interaction .



- **[ATutor](https://github.com/atutor/ATutor)**  

  Web-based LMS for developing and delivering online courses, with accessibility features and theme customization .



- **[Forma LMS](https://github.com/formalms/formalms)**  

  Corporate-oriented LMS with certification tracking, reporting, and SCORM support.



- **[Kolibri](https://github.com/learningequality/kolibri)**  

  Offline-first learning platform designed for low-bandwidth and low-resource environments .



- **[LearnPress](https://github.com/LearnPress/learnpress)**  

  WordPress LMS plugin for creating and selling courses within an existing WordPress site .



- **[LifterLMS](https://github.com/gocodebox/lifterlms)**  

  WordPress-based LMS for building engaging online courses with e-commerce and protection features .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Marsha** — Open-source LTI Learning Content Management System (LCMS) for video-centric learning .

- **Ralph** — Learning Record Store (LRS) and analytics toolkit for xAPI data .

- **Warren** — Visualization toolkit for learning analytics with xAPI support .

- **Joanie** — Headless ERP for course enrollment, payment, and certificate delivery .

- **Open edX Docker** — Best-practice Docker image for deploying Open edX .

- Many community **xAPI/LRS** and **LTI** tools for extending any LMS.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Moodle** or **Open edX** with **Richie** for course portals, **Ralph** for learning analytics, and **Docker Compose** for reproducible self-hosted deployments.
